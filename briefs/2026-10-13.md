## Where the puck is going

The new execs' framing matches the field's open problems. Two of the three barriers it names, reasoning speed and recognition, are the intelligence-latency tradeoff and accuracy-on-hard-audio threads tracked since June. The full-duplex point is the useful part: Thinking Machines and StepFun showed architectures, but operators running voice at scale say the user-visible leap hasn't landed. Treat full-duplex as promising, not yet a buying criterion.

The practical read for a builder: customers will keep judging you on whether the call went right. The Oct 8 all-in-cost critique, the recovery-loop thread and this piece all point the same way. Differentiate on reliability in a specific vertical (accuracy on names and numbers, graceful handoff), not on another point of TTS latency.

With the post-DevDay and Microsoft release wave settled, news flow is thin. Expect independent benchmarks and further platform M&A to be the next movers.
