## Where the puck is going

**The latency story just picked up a compute-layer data point that voice founders should file even though it isn't a voice product.** This brief has spent three months tracking the interactivity-as-architecture thesis — Thinking Machines and OpenAI both arguing that decoupling "always listening" from "always computing" is the real unlock, not raw model quality. Jalapeño's first benchmarks are the infrastructure argument underneath that thesis made concrete: OpenAI built a chip specifically around the latency profile of interactive agents, and it's shipping 1.7–3.6x faster on exactly that workload class. If that gap holds once deployed, the floor under "what latency is achievable" moves for every S2S model sitting on frontier-lab compute — a different lever than the STT/TTS-layer optimization this brief has tracked all summer (Deepgram Flux, ElevenLabs' turn-detection tuning). Worth watching whether Cartesia, Deepgram, or ElevenLabs say anything about their own inference-hardware roadmap in response.

**Four days of silence on Murf's price cut is itself a signal, not just an absence of one.** Every prior TTS leaderboard flip this brief has tracked (six since June) triggered visible movement — a competing launch, a changelog update, a pricing page edit — within days. A fifth of ElevenLabs' price with comparable latency claims sitting unanswered for four days reads one of two ways: either the premium vendors have quietly concluded price isn't the axis their customers buy on (voice quality, latency consistency, and orchestration features remain the moat), or they're still deciding how to respond and the next few days matter. Either reading is useful to a founder pricing a new TTS integration right now.

**Everything else this brief has been tracking is in the same holding pattern it was in on Aug 31.** The bundle-into-the-platform thread (Mavenir, Krisp, Speechmatics) hasn't added a fourth node. The cascade-vs-S2S architecture debate hasn't moved. Cartesia's Sonic-3.6 GA is done and settled. The news cycle this week is genuinely thinner than the last two.
