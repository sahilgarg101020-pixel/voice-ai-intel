## Where the puck is going

**Capital is moving to vertical voice and the tooling around it, away from foundation voice models.** Karta targets BFSI collections and servicing, with India as the first market. VoiceRun sells the build-and-run layer. That fits the earlier funding pattern in healthcare and mortgage voice (Vocca, Sela). If you're building horizontally, vertical incumbents are now being seeded in every regulated segment, and they win on compliance and workflow depth rather than on the model.

**The speech-to-speech debate is settling into "S2S for latency, cascade for control."** One vendor analysis in this cycle argues cascaded pipelines still win in production. That's consistent with the ElevenLabs Eleven Realtime critique (Oct 23) that you get no transcript and no component control. The practical frontier is hybrid: S2S for the conversational turn, with transcripts and logs kept as a side channel for audit.

**Latency targets are converging.** NVIDIA's guidance puts the target under one second from end of speech to first audio, and reports about 0.79s for its blueprint. Table-stakes is now around 1s end to end, so sub-300ms claims are differentiating only if they hold under tool calls and retrieval.
