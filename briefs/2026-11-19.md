## TLDR

- **AssemblyAI moved its speech-to-text line to the STT-3.7 family.** It has three variants: batch, Realtime, and a **Realtime-Agent** model for voice agents priced at **$0.45/hr**. AssemblyAI reports 4.46% WER on 12,460 English voice-agent calls, against 5.19% for Universal-3.6 Pro Realtime, which is a vendor-reported 14% drop. It covers 32 languages with auto-detect. Migrating only means changing the model name. Universal-3.5 Pro was deprecated Nov 17. Accounts that don't set `speech_models` are reportedly moved to 3.7 automatically on **Dec 8**, and 3.5 Pro Realtime retires Feb 1, 2027 ([AssemblyAI changelog](https://assemblyai.com/changelog)).
- **ElevenLabs launched a Marketplace (Nov 11) for industry-specific agents built on its stack.** Launch partners are Salesforce (Agentforce voice), IBM, NiCE, Twilio and Zendesk. Listings cover healthcare, financial services, insurance and retail. For now it is a discovery and lead layer, with no listed pricing and no independent performance data ([ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/marketplace), [LetsDataScience](https://letsdatascience.com/news/elevenlabs-launches-marketplace-for-industry-specific-ai-age-49a2e8cf)).
- **Google added 66 regional voices to the Gemini app (Nov 16).** They cover US, UK and Indian English, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian and Korean, and Google says they're tuned for "natural cadences" rather than exaggerated accents ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/regional-voices/), [Android Headlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/11/google-gemini-66-new-regional-voices.html)).
- **Stork benchmarked 10 speech-to-text models on G.711 phone audio and found five tied at 5.5–6.0% WER, within the margin of error.** The leader, gpt-transcribe, got 97.5% of names, digits and addresses right at $0.27/hr. Background noise cost most models 0.4–2.0 WER points, while 8kHz audio cost the best ones ≤0.3. It tested batch only, not streaming ([Stork](https://www.stork.ai/blog/speech-to-text-phone-audio-test-2026)).
- **Minor:** Perplexity reportedly added voice control of its AI teammates on mobile (Nov 18): assigning projects, getting updates and redirecting work by voice. This comes from a single source and the details are unverified ([Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/perplexity-brings-voice-controlled-ai-teammates-to-mobile/)).

## Where the puck is going

**Raw WER has stopped separating speech-to-text vendors.** Stork found five models within noise of each other on phone audio. AssemblyAI is now headlining a *voice-agent-call* WER rather than a general one. The tracker theme that STT competes on "finished output" holds up: the useful numbers are entity accuracy (names, digits, addresses) and noise robustness, not 8kHz telephony. That matches Bland pitching Tofu-1 on separating the caller from room noise last week. If you pick STT on a leaderboard WER, you are optimizing the wrong variable. Build a 200-call eval scored on entities from your own traffic.

**Silent auto-migrations are now an operational risk.** AssemblyAI's Dec 8 default flip joins Cartesia Line (Dec 1), ChatGPT legacy voice (Dec 11) and Vertex native audio (Dec 13), plus Hume's shutdown last week. Four vendors change underlying models in under two weeks. Even a better model shifts entity formatting, punctuation and end-of-turn timing, and that breaks downstream parsers and prompts. Pin model versions explicitly and regression-test before each date. There is still room for a tool that diffs a vendor's model swap against your own recorded calls.

**The platforms are also turning into marketplaces.** The tracker has followed independent agents distributed through incumbents' marketplaces (Quiq/Synthflow on Zoom, Cresta into Agentforce). ElevenLabs is now doing the reverse: it hosts the app layer itself, and Deepgram's 19-partner marketplace follows the same pattern. For vertical-agent startups, a listing is cheap distribution, but it puts your agent next to Zendesk's and Salesforce's own agents. If you list, differentiate on workflow depth, not voice quality.

**Accents are table stakes, and the competition is moving to regionalized voices.** Google's 66 voices target India, Brazil, Indonesia and Korea, the same markets ElevenLabs is chasing with the Kotoba deal and its India push. Consumer users will come to expect locally natural voices before most enterprise agent stacks offer them.

## Signals from the field

- **Benchmark:** Stork found that 8kHz phone audio costs top models "0.3 points or less" of WER, while noise costs up to 2 points, so fix the noise before the codec ([Stork](https://www.stork.ai/blog/speech-to-text-phone-audio-test-2026)).
- **Skeptic (Tell-1, selectable today):** OrcaRouter says Retell's #1 ranking comes from a benchmark "the company wrote itself," and that Tell-1 can't be taken outside Retell. It recommends testing it against a frontier model on your own calls ([OrcaRouter](https://www.orcarouter.ai/sv/blog/retell-tell-1-voice-model)).
- **Community:** HN commenters say tool calls add "500ms or more" on top of base latency, and that turn-taking still causes more friction than raw speed ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48013919)).
