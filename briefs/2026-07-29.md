# Voice AI Brief — 2026-07-29

*A quiet cycle for headline model launches, but two stories point at the same trend: everyone adjacent to voice AI wants to own more of the stack — vendors are extending TTS into full audio-native platforms, and global systems integrators are lining up to own enterprise voice-AI delivery.*

## TLDR

- **Fish Audio raises $52M seed** (Coreline Ventures, Capital Today co-lead), disclosing $21M ARR and 8M+ users a year after starting as a bedroom project. It's using the round to push beyond TTS into voice-native LLMs and speech-to-speech — the same "own the full audio stack" move Cartesia (Sonic+Ink+Line, July 2) and ElevenLabs (Speech Engine, June) have already made. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/28/fish-audio-raises-50m-seed-to-build-ai-voice-models-for-creators-and-enterprises/), [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/28/fish-audio-makes-splash-raising-52m-seed-funding-ai-voices/))
- **DXC Technology becomes ElevenLabs' second major global-SI partner in five weeks**, following TELUS Digital (June 24). DXC also invested in ElevenLabs' Series D and will co-develop voice solutions through its LabX incubator for its enterprise customer base. ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/dxc-and-elevenlabs-announce-strategic-partnership-to-scale-enterprise-ai-and-voice-innovation-302835490.html))
- **Infra-layer cooldown hits a 13th straight week**: still nothing headline-grade from Cartesia, Deepgram, AssemblyAI, Speechmatics, or Soniox — though Deepgram continues shipping small changelog items (numerals formatting, monolingual model refreshes) without a named release.
- **EU AI Act Article 50** (deepfake/synthetic-voice disclosure, fines up to €15M or 3% global turnover) becomes enforceable in four days, August 2.

## Where the puck is going

**Every layer of voice AI is trying to annex the layer next to it, and Fish Audio is the clearest new data point.** A company that built its name on open, community-driven TTS (Fish-Speech, 2m+ cloned voices) is now explicitly funding its way into voice-native LLMs and speech-to-speech — the same full-stack ambition Cartesia and ElevenLabs have already acted on this cycle. What makes Fish Audio's move notable isn't the ambition itself (that's now default founder behavior) but its customer list: OpenAI, HeyGen, Retell, and LiveKit all reportedly use Fish Audio's models today, which means some of Fish's own customers are simultaneously racing to build the native voice capability Fish is trying to sell them. That's the actual shape of "stack consolidation" in this market right now — not clean vertical integration, but overlapping coopetition where yesterday's supplier is tomorrow's competitor.

**The DXC/ElevenLabs deal is the second data point (after TELUS) in what's shaping up as a land grab among global SIs for the enterprise voice-delivery layer**, not just a vendor-partner press release. Two of the largest systems integrators committing to a single voice-AI startup's platform inside five weeks suggests SIs see enterprise voice deployment — not the underlying model — as where the durable services revenue sits, mirroring how the same firms built businesses around SAP or Salesforce integration rather than the software itself. Worth watching whether Accenture, Deloitte, or Cognizant follow, and whether any of them hedge by pairing with a second voice vendor rather than going exclusive.
