## Where the puck is going

**Cartesia's Sonic-3.6 is the fifth different #1 TTS model this brief has tracked since June** (Alibaba Fun-Realtime-TTS, Grok TTS on Vapi, MiniMax Speech 2.8, Speechify Simba 3.2, now Cartesia), which keeps confirming the standing thesis: model-quality leadership on a TTS leaderboard has a shelf life measured in weeks, not quarters. What's more interesting is what Cartesia bundled it with — a simultaneous Ink-2 refresh, not a standalone TTS drop. That's the same instinct ElevenLabs has been running since June (pair every model win with session-stack lock-in: STT + TTS + turn detection under one roof) rather than betting the model alone holds the line. Worth noting this ships in beta, API-only, docs still defaulting to 3.5 — a reminder that "#1 on the leaderboard" and "what's actually running in production" are two different dates.

**Deepgram's Singapore HQ and ElevenLabs' Singapore data residency landing in the same week is a real pattern, not noise.** This brief has tracked the regional/sovereign-voice-AI thread since June — Poland's equity stake in ElevenLabs, India's Gnani AI backed by the IndiaAI Mission, Deepgram's own Australia endpoint reaching GA in June — and Southeast Asia is now visibly next. Deepgram's 96%-YoY APAC growth number is the more concrete tell here than the HQ announcement itself: regional demand was already outpacing regional infrastructure, and EDBI's investment reads as Singapore actively courting that gap the way Poland courted ElevenLabs. For a founder building outside the US/EU, this is the clearest signal yet that "which region do I deploy voice infra in" is becoming a real strategic choice with capital behind it, not just a compliance checkbox.

**ElevenLabs quietly defaulting ElevenAgents' ASR to its own `scribe_realtime` is the completion of a pattern, not a new one.** Scribe v2 Realtime shipped as an opt-in STT option in June; by August it's the default. Combined with the Flows API and turn/soft-timeout controls, ElevenLabs keeps tightening the loop where every new agent feature reinforces the reason to stay on ElevenLabs' own stack rather than swap in a third-party STT — the same "default voice wrapper" dynamic this brief flagged around the Anthropic Cookbook endorsement in June, now showing up as a quiet default-flip instead of an external endorsement.
