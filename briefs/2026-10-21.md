## Where the puck is going

**A frontier-lab-adjacent open vendor now sells the whole stack.** Voxtral 3 pairs STT, TTS and an agent SDK. This follows the pattern of vendors bundling the pipeline (LiveKit/xAI, Microsoft MAI, Grok Voice) so builders buy one API key. Mistral's earlier Voxtral TTS (March) was a model; this adds the orchestration layer. For a founder, a bundled stack from a well-funded European lab tightens the squeeze on thin orchestration layers.

**STT pricing keeps falling toward a floor.** At $0.008/min streaming and $0.0035/min batch, transcription is a rounding error next to LLM and telephony costs. That fits the all-in-cost gap thread: STT price is no longer the lever, and differentiation moves to error-class accuracy (medical terms, digits, negations) and turn detection.

**The comparison against Whisper large-v3 is a weak baseline.** Beating a 2023 model on FLEURS says little about noisy 8kHz phone audio, which is what agents hear. Expect the test that matters to be agent-specific sets like AA-AgentTalk.
