## Where the puck is going

**ElevenLabs is running a two-tier play against the commoditizing model layer.** Alibaba's 70–95% price cuts (Sept 28 brief) and the summer's TTS leaderboard churn say raw TTS is a race to the bottom. ElevenLabs' response is the opposite of a price cut: a premium expressive flagship (priced to double v3 after promo) paired with a ~100ms Turbo for agents at half the launch price, both bundled into ElevenAgents. That continues the "default voice wrapper" entrenchment thread — quality and emotion sold to creators, latency sold to agent builders, and the stack sold as one. For a founder: if you sit on a third-party TTS, expect ElevenLabs to be both your best-quality option and the most expensive lock-in.

**"Finished output" is replacing "transcript" as the STT product.** AssemblyAI's Dictation API returns what the user meant to say, not what they said. Combined with Wispr's dictation raise in August and Speechmatics' agent-specific STT semantic-error focus earlier this month, the STT vendors are competing on downstream usefulness (self-correction resolution, custom vocab, semantic error rate) rather than WER. WER is table-stakes; post-processing quality is the new differentiator.

**Supervision is becoming its own funded layer.** Modulate's round, on top of its Ai4 "voice agent supervision" pitch and this brief's recurring recovery-loop theme (Sinch's rollback data, false-interruption CSAT findings), suggests investors see runtime monitoring — frustration, fraud, deepfake, compliance — as a distinct category from the agent platform itself. Regulated verticals (mortgage, health) are the pull; if you're deploying agents there, buyers will increasingly ask what watches the agent.

## Signals from the field

- **Community critique (recurring):** developer write-ups keep landing on the same two failures — 1–2 seconds of audio already "in flight" when a user barges in, and backchannels ("uh-huh") wrongly killing the agent mid-sentence. Interruption quality, not raw latency, is where builders say agents still feel robotic. ([AssemblyAI on agent challenges](https://www.assemblyai.com/blog/biggest-challenges-building-ai-voice-agents-how-assemblyai-vapi-are-solving-them))
