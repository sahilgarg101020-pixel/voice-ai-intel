## Where the puck is going

**Voice is becoming a thin front-end on an agent backend, and the backend just got cheaper.** GPT-Live-1's split (a cheap flat-rate conversational layer, with reasoning billed separately) meets DevDay's price cut on agentic intelligence. The strategic read: the "voice layer" is priced like telephony ($3/hr) while the value and margin live in the agent that acts. Builders competing on the conversational shell alone are competing against a $0.05/min floor; differentiation moves to actions, domain state, and recovery — the same recovery-loop theme this brief has tracked.

**Outbound is the next contested surface for incumbents.** Aircall joins the wave of platforms adding outbound voice campaigns. Outbound raises the stakes on compliance, disclosure, and supervision (see Modulate, Pindrop's agent detection earlier this month): more autonomy means buyers ask harder questions about who watches the agent.

**Voice as a control surface for other agents keeps spreading.** From Devin Voice to Codex voice and ChatGPT's Work tab, the pattern is voice steering long-running agents. Dots (always-on, working between conversations) make the next step obvious: proactive voice — the agent calling *you* with status or an approval request. Nobody has clearly shipped that as a voice-native product yet.
