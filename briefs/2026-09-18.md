## TLDR

- **StepFun ships StepAudio 3 Realtime** (Sept 16–17): a five-model audio family that tops Artificial Analysis on both Conversational Dynamics (98.9%) and Speech Reasoning (99.7%), with ASR at 1.7% WER tying the leaderboard's best. Headline feature is "Think-While-Speaking" — private reasoning executed in parallel with spoken delivery, claimed comparable to dedicated reasoning models while still speaking in real time. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14005), [StepFun](https://stepaudiollm.github.io/step-audio-3-realtime/))
- **Treble raises $18M Series A extension** (Reykjavik, Paladin Capital lead, Sept 16): its platform generates synthetic voice interactions to stress-test STT/TTS/voice-agent stacks under real acoustic conditions — room noise, reflections, mic quality — without paying humans to record test audio. Customers include Amazon and Logitech; the company is now pushing the same acoustic-simulation layer into robotics, automotive, and drones. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/16/iceland-based-treble-raises-18-million-for-its-voice-simulation-platform/))
- **ElevenLabs ships Scribe v2 Medical to GA** (Sept 11): a HIPAA-eligible batch STT model specialized for clinician-patient conversations, dictation, and intake/care-coordination calls, billed at the same rate as Scribe v2. ([ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/9/11))
- **Egma, an open-source voice-agent testing/simulation platform, gains real Hacker News traction**: self-hosted regression suites defined as markdown, graded simulations across LiveKit and Retell — a week after Cloudflare shipped native per-turn diagnostics into its own SDK (Sept 11), a second signal the named testing-tooling category (Bluejay/Coval/Cekura/Maxim/Hamming) is fragmenting toward free/self-hosted options. ## Where the puck is going

**Two frontier labs landed the same architectural bet on live-speech reasoning within 48 hours of each other, which is a stronger signal than either alone.** Google's Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking (Sept 15, reported yesterday) reasons *during* the audio stream rather than forcing a fast-shallow-vs-slow-smart choice. StepAudio 3 Realtime's "Think-While-Speaking" does the same thing from a different lab, in the same week, executing private reasoning in parallel with spoken output instead of pausing to think. The intelligence-latency tradeoff this brief has tracked since the June Coval/Pipecat conversation isn't just getting faster inference on the old curve — two independent teams are now shipping a genuinely different architecture for it in the same cycle.

**StepAudio 3 also extends a thread worth naming explicitly: Chinese labs are now a real presence at the top of Western speech leaderboards, not an occasional entrant.** After Alibaba's Fun-Realtime-TTS (June) and MiniMax's Speech 2.8 (June), StepFun is the third Chinese lab this cycle to top an Artificial Analysis speech sub-leaderboard. Founders benchmarking vendors should treat this as a standing category, not a novelty — leave a Chinese-lab model out of a bake-off and you're comparing against a shrinking slice of the frontier.

**Treble's raise is a quieter data point in the audio-testing-infra layer this brief has tracked since the named testing-tooling category emerged in June.** It sits upstream of conversation-level scoring (Coval, Hamming, Cekura): synthetic acoustic conditions — the noisy kitchen, the echoey lobby, the cheap headset mic — rather than the words themselves. Pairing that with a push into robotics, automotive, and drones is a bet that "how does this system hold up against real acoustic noise" outlives conversational voice agents as a business. Egma's Hacker News traction the same week is the open-source mirror image of the same instinct, arriving days after Cloudflare folded equivalent diagnostics directly into its Agents SDK — three different shapes (funded acoustic simulation, open-source self-hosted testing, native platform diagnostics) converging on the same layer in under two weeks.
