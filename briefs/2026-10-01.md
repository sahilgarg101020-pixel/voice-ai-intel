## Where the puck is going

**Model vendors are buying their way up to the agent layer.** Inworld (speech models, TTS-2) acquiring Ultravox (an S2S platform) follows the pattern this brief has tracked in ElevenLabs bundling STT, MCP and agents into one stack. The pure-model business is hard to defend when TTS leadership flips every few weeks, so the defensible position is owning the session: orchestration, telephony hooks and the speech model together. Expect more tuck-ins of small agent platforms by model labs, and treat "independent platform on someone else's voices" as a shrinking niche.

**The LLM step in the pipeline is being attacked on latency.** Mercury Voice is a bet that the cascaded STT→LLM→TTS pipeline survives if the middle step gets fast and cheap enough, as opposed to full-duplex S2S such as GPT-Live-1. A ~320ms first-token figure is only the LLM slice, though. Last month's real-call measurements found vendor latency claims ran about 490ms better than what was measured on live calls. Until someone publishes end-to-end numbers on a real phone path, treat it as a promising component and not a proven win.

**Capital is flowing to the leader and to the sovereign-stack layer, not to the middle.** ElevenLabs' tender at $22B, with no new money raised, signals employee liquidity at a mature stage while the independent middle consolidates.
