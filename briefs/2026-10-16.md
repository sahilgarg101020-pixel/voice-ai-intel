## Where the puck is going

The Vapi limitations list shows what speech-to-speech still lacks for production. The model is fast and natural, but fallbacks, handoffs and custom voices are the pieces enterprise buyers ask about in procurement. Pipelines still win on control and recovery, which matches the earlier theme that the moat is the recovery loop, not the model. A platform that adds fallbacks and handoffs around a speech-to-speech core would have a real edge over one that only exposes the model.

The Hume news, if confirmed, is a vendor-risk lesson. Hume was the best-known name in emotion-aware voice, and Google hired its founder in September. If the standalone API is going away, expressive voice is being absorbed into big-lab models, and independent specialists need either a hard-to-copy wedge or an acquirer. For founders, it's a reason to keep a swap path on every voice dependency.

Prices keep falling, so the open question is where margin lives. Developer-facing value is moving to actions, state and compliance in one vertical.
