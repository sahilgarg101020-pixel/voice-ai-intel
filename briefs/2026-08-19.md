## Where the puck is going

**Reddit's Play mode is a useful data point on where TTS shows up next, even though it's not a builder-facing product.** It's the second major consumer platform this year to treat "read it to me" as a first-class feature rather than an accessibility afterthought — and Huffman's own framing (built specifically to compete with third-party Reddit-narration accounts that were already siphoning engagement to TikTok) is the tell. When narration quality gets cheap and fast enough that a platform builds it in-house to defend engagement rather than license it out, that's the signal the utterance-level TTS market has actually reached the "good enough, ship it everywhere" threshold this brief has been tracking since the open-source TTS flood in June. The interesting unknown, not yet answered anywhere in coverage: whose model is under the hood. Reddit hasn't said, and none of the usual vendor-attribution channels (release notes, vendor case-study pages) have claimed it yet — worth checking again next cycle.

**The ElevenLabs update matters more for what it says about where the interruption-handling fight has moved than for the feature itself.** This brief has tracked false-interruption rate as a bigger lever than raw WER since the June 12 clinic-builder signal (cutting false interruptions from 4.2 to 1.1 per call moved CSAT 22 points; WER optimization barely moved it) and ElevenLabs' own `interruption_ignore_terms` release in June (suppressing end-of-turn detection on known phrases like disclaimers). Background audio filtering is the natural next layer: instead of telling the model which *phrases* to ignore, filter which *sound sources* count as speech in the first place — nearby conversations, other TVs, other calls in a noisy contact-center floor. That three-release arc (turn-detection tuning → phrase suppression → source filtering) is a cleaner picture of an unglamorous but high-value engineering roadmap than any single launch would suggest, and it's a reminder that this class of fix ships quietly in a changelog, not a press release — this one sat unreported for eight days until this cycle's direct changelog check caught it.
