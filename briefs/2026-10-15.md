## Where the puck is going

Healthcare scheduling keeps attracting vertical-voice money. Vocca follows the pattern in Sela (mortgage), Revin (roofing) and the earlier Bland/Retell rounds: investors fund agents tied to a measurable workflow, such as booked appointments, instead of general-purpose platforms. Vocca's pitch is "scheduling infrastructure", which positions it as a system of record for practice operations, not a call-handling layer.

For a founder, the takeaway is the same as in recent cycles. Horizontal agent platforms face bundling and price cuts (the Alibaba, Microsoft and Meta price moves). The defensible position is integration depth and outcome data in one regulated vertical. Healthcare adds compliance and EHR-integration moats, which also connect to the offline/air-gapped deployment thread from yesterday.

News flow remains thin after the post-DevDay and Microsoft release wave. Independent benchmarks and platform M&A are still the likeliest next movers.
