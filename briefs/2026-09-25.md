## Where the puck is going

**Sela is the clearest production-economics data point this brief has seen from the vertical-voice-agent wave.** Where most vertical agent raises (Bland, Retell, Vapi and peers closing $50–250M rounds) lead with call volume or valuation, Sela leads with a controlled A/B result — a 9% lead-to-lock lift and 40%+ profit uplift against the lender's existing process, at $1B+/month in real originations across 6 of the 10 largest independent mortgage banks. For a founder picking a vertical, that's the bar: not "we handle X calls," but a measurable revenue delta an enterprise buyer can underwrite before committing budget.

**OpenAI keeps extending voice from "conversation" into "control surface for other agents," and it's now doing it on the surface with the most users.** This brief flagged GPT-Live's desktop rollout in July as letting users steer Codex and ChatGPT Work agents by voice mid-conversation — a control-surface use, not just a chat one. Bringing that same instinct (voice triggers a document draft, an email, a Slack summary) to the mobile Work tab, gated behind Plus/Pro, suggests OpenAI sees voice-driven agent invocation as a paid-tier differentiator rather than a free commodity feature — worth watching whether Google (Workspace Live) or Anthropic (Claude voice + tool integrations) match the paywall placement, not just the capability.

**Alebex AI's benchmark debut is a reminder that the STT leaderboard is still permeable to new entrants, not just an incumbent-vs-Chinese-lab story.** This brief has tracked Alibaba, MiniMax, and StepFun taking #1 spots on Western leaderboards all summer; Alebex cracking the top 10 against Google/Microsoft/NVIDIA/Amazon on a first release is the same permeability playing out from a different direction — a small, unknown team can still out-benchmark hyperscalers on pure accuracy. As always with a first-release, self-selected leaderboard placement: treat it as a lead worth an independent eval, not a settled result, until there's a second model or a named production customer behind it.
