## Where the puck is going

**"Bundle into the platform, don't sell the component" now spans three distinct layers of the stack in two weeks.** Mavenir shipped Sanas' deepfake detection into the telecom core (Aug 26). Krisp shipped noise cancellation and accent conversion into 8x8's CX suite (Aug 28). Now Speechmatics is shipping STT into LiveKit's agent-orchestration marketplace — no separate account, no separate bill. Three different component categories (fraud detection, audio cleanup, transcription) all reaching the same conclusion at the same moment: distribution through whoever the developer is already paying beats a standalone API. For a founder choosing a stack, this means the component-vendor-vs-platform decision is disappearing — the platform increasingly *is* the marketplace.

**A dedicated voice-model router arriving now isn't a coincidence — it's what six leaderboard flips since June produces.** Alibaba, Grok/Vapi, MiniMax, Speechify, and twice now Cartesia have each taken the #1 TTS spot this cycle, each within weeks of the last. Speko betting a company on "route to whichever model is best today, benchmarked the day it ships" is a direct read that picking one vendor and committing is now the riskier move than staying provider-agnostic — the same shape as the LLM-router category that emerged once text-model leaderboards started churning weekly.

**Cartesia's execution matters more than the model itself.** A GA ship that lands inside a self-declared window is rare enough in this category (this brief has tracked multiple quiet slips) that it's worth treating as a signal in its own right: for a founder evaluating vendors, "did they say what they'd do and then do it" is a data point orthogonal to Elo.

## Signals from the field

- **A useful corrective to the speech-to-speech narrative**: engineering write-ups from AssemblyAI and Gradium both make the case that a well-engineered cascade (STT→LLM→TTS) can beat end-to-end S2S models on raw latency, and remains the dominant production architecture for anything needing tool-calling, compliance, or auditability — S2S is still framed as research/prototype-stage for enterprise use. Worth weighing against vendor S2S marketing before defaulting to it.
