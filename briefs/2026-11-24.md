## TLDR

- **Innovaccer put xAI's Grok Voice (speech-to-speech) live in its Gravity Voice healthcare agents (Nov 23).** Existing customers get it first through upgrades, and a HIPAA BAA is available. SiliconANGLE says xAI now operates as "SpaceXSI." Booking rules and eligibility logic stay in Innovaccer's own systems. Unite.ai notes that Innovaccer published no before/after production numbers ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/23/innovaccer-expands-gravity-voice-ai-agents-with-spacexsis-grok-voice-model/), [Unite.ai](https://www.unite.ai/innovaccer-adds-grok-voice-to-healthcare-phone-agents/), [Fierce Healthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/ai-and-machine-learning/innovaccer-integrates-grok-voice-gravity-voice-ai-agents)).
- **Natural launched Natural Voice (Nov 23): your agent creates a payment request and transfers the caller to Natural's own payment agent, then leaves the call.** That agent confirms the total, takes the card verbally and charges it, so card data never touches your stack. After three failed attempts it texts a checkout link instead. It follows Natural Accept (Nov 19) and a $30M Series A ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/natural-enables-voice-agents-to-accept-payments-over-the-phone/), [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/natural-bags-30m-launches-agentic-payments-infrastructure)).
- **OpenAI shipped GPT-Live-1.1 to the API with a $0.02/min "standby" rate, compared with $0.05/min when active (Nov 19).** Standby covers idle WebRTC sessions with a 5–60s idle timeout and also applies to GPT-Live-1. Backend model and tool tokens are billed separately ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/21/openai-gpt-live-1-1-api-standby-mode/), [i-scoop](https://www.i-scoop.eu/gpt-live-1-1-api/)).
- **Inworld open-sourced SAVA (Apache 2.0, Nov 18), a single model that does both voice-activity detection and end-of-turn detection.** It runs under 3ms per 40ms frame on one CPU thread and claims a 71ms median detection delay against 210ms for smart-turn. On LiveKit's independent 14-language eot-bench it lands mid-pack, between ultraVAD and SmartTurn v3.2 ([Inworld](https://inworld.ai/blog/introducing-sava), [LiveKit eot-bench PR](https://github.com/livekit/eot-bench/pull/31)).
- **Crunchbase counts $9.5B raised by voice AI companies in 2026 through Nov 11, up 67% on 2025, across only 154 deals (273 in 2025).** Big rounds to infrastructure companies with broader businesses, such as Fireworks AI's $1.5B, inflate the total ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/voice-ai-startups-global-investment), [Artiverse](https://www.artiverse.ca/voice-ais-billion-dollar-boom-reveals-a-sharper-startup-divide/)).

## Where the puck is going

**Speech-to-speech is being swapped in as a replaceable component.** Innovaccer adding Grok Voice, which reads like an addition to its existing stack rather than a full switch, follows NICE Cognigy's native-S2S move and Microsoft's 1¢/min MAI Realtime. Platforms that own the vertical logic (eligibility, scheduling rules, EHR writes) now treat the S2S model as an interchangeable voice layer. The tracker's "thin voice front-end, value in actions and state" theme keeps getting confirmed. For builders, the defensible asset is the action layer and the compliance wrapper (a BAA, audit logs), not the choice of voice model.

**The voice agent is starting to hand off to other agents.** Natural doesn't try to make the merchant's agent PCI-compliant. It moves the caller to a separate, specialized agent that has its own compliance scope. That is the same pattern as a human transfer, but between agents, and it extends last cycle's "the handoff is the product" theme. Expect identity verification, consent capture and TCPA opt-out (effective tomorrow) to arrive as hosted agents you transfer callers to. Making your agent a clean transfer source and target, with context passed along and a return path, becomes a feature.

**Pricing is changing to match how long sessions actually last.** A separate standby rate tells developers OpenAI expects always-on voice sessions (companions, copilots, in-app agents) where most minutes are silence. Model idle time explicitly in your unit economics. A per-active-minute price comparison is now misleading.

**Turn detection keeps becoming a free, open component.** SAVA joins Pipecat's classifiers and Twilio's GA turn model. Its middling independent score is the useful part: vendor delay numbers and multilingual bench results disagree, so run your own eot-bench before you switch.

## Signals from the field

- **Developer complaint:** an OpenAI community thread reports that GPT-Live "sometimes never delegates (acknowledges verbally, sends nothing)." That failure, an agent saying it will act and then not calling the tool, is worse than latency, and an eval should catch it ([OpenAI Community](https://community.openai.com/tag/gpt-live/1198)).
- **Healthcare skeptic:** Unite.ai points out that the 5M+ patient interactions Innovaccer cites predate Grok, so there is no evidence yet that the model swap moved containment ([Unite.ai](https://www.unite.ai/innovaccer-adds-grok-voice-to-healthcare-phone-agents/)).
- **Customer outcome:** Bevado customer Zest Properties reports a 300%+ increase in leasing showings. This is a vendor-supplied number ([Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/bevado-raises-10-million-seed-round-for-property-management-ai-agents/)).
