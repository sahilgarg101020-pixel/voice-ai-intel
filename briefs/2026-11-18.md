## TLDR

- **Microsoft announced MAI Realtime 1 at Ignite (Nov 17), its first speech-to-speech model.** Pricing starts at **$0.01 per audio minute**, and reasoning tokens are billed separately (about $5 per 1M output tokens). It is compatible with the OpenAI Realtime API. Microsoft says it beats GPT-Realtime 2.1 on quality, speed and price. It is a limited preview through MAI Labs, and Artificial Analysis has not scored it yet ([Microsoft AI](https://microsoft.ai/news/introducing-mai-realtime-1/), [DEV](https://dev.to/alifar/microsoft-mai-realtime-1-launches-in-preview-with-001-audio-minute-pricing-189), [OrcaRouter](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/realtime-1-vs-gpt-live-1)). Microsoft also launched **Dragon Copilot Transcribe**, built on MAI-Transcribe-2, and says it now handles all doctor–patient transcription in Dragon Copilot ([Microsoft AI](https://microsoft.ai/news/microsoft-ignite-2026/)).
- **Retell launched Tell-1, its own language model for contact-center calls.** It starts speaking while it is still reasoning ("interleaved reasoning"). Retell claims under 100ms model latency, priced at $0.035/min, and it becomes selectable Nov 19. Tell-1 ranks #1 on Retell's own vCX benchmark but trails other models on grounding, instruction following and tool calling. There is no model card ([Retell](https://www.retellai.com/tell-1-technical-blog), [Smart Customer Service](https://www.smartcustomerservice.com/Articles/News-Briefs/Retell-Launches-Tell-1-a-Contact-Center-LLM-177514.aspx), [OrcaRouter critique](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/retell-tell-1-voice-model)).
- **Bland released a research preview of Tofu-1 (Nov 15), its first full-duplex speech-to-speech model.** Bland pitches it on separating the caller's voice from TVs, children and road noise. Access is limited to enterprises in banking, healthcare, insurance and government, with general availability planned for January. There are no published latency, pricing or benchmark numbers ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/15/bland-tofu-1-full-duplex-speech-to-speech-model/), [Glitchwire](https://glitchwire.com/news/bland-claims-tofu-1-can-hear-a-caller-through-a-crying-baby-and-a-highway/)).
- **Fish Audio launched Fish Agent in public beta.** It is a no-code phone-agent builder on its full-duplex Live 3 model, which is English-only at launch. It offers rented numbers or bring-your-own Twilio numbers, plus an on-premises option ([Fish docs](https://docs.fish.audio/agents/overview), [AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/fish-audio-s-live-3-builds-a-voice-ai-agent-in-30-seconds)).

## Where the puck is going

**The speech layer is heading toward a cent a minute.** GPT-Live set $0.05/min in September and Cartesia Managed Agents cut to $0.03. Microsoft now lists $0.01 for an OpenAI-compatible speech-to-speech model. All three bill reasoning separately. That turns voice into the transport layer, and the margin moves to the reasoning tokens and the workflow. Because the Realtime protocol is compatible, switching providers is mostly a config change, so a cheaper model can be benchmarked right away. If your gross margin depends on reselling audio minutes, assume that line goes to zero within two quarters. Price on outcomes, as SquadStack does, or on workflow ownership.

**Agent platforms are building their own models.** Retell (Tell-1), Bland (Tofu-1), Fish (Live 3) and, earlier, Decagon (Chord), Guava and Phonic are all moving off third-party models. The story is the same each time: millions of their own calls become a model tuned for phone audio, interruptions and contact-center language. It supports the tracker theme that the moat is the customer-data loop rather than the base model, but every claim is self-benchmarked. Tell-1 wins on latency and colloquial speech and loses on tool calling, which is the core of an enterprise agent. Buyers will start asking for third-party evals such as Coval and Artificial Analysis. If you build evals, scoring vendor in-house models on tool-calling and grounding under noisy phone audio is open space.

**Noise robustness is the new differentiator for phone speech-to-speech.** Tofu-1's whole pitch is hearing the caller, not the room. Taken with last week's finding that barge-in failures are behind many "latency" complaints, the frontier for phone speech-to-speech is selective attention: whose speech counts as a turn. Native-audio models can make that call from the audio itself, but nobody has published a benchmark for it yet.

## Signals from the field

- **Builder take:** LiveKit's Jesse Hall says speed alone doesn't make an agent feel human. LiveKit's audio-based turn detector cut callers off 4.5% of the time, against 9.9% for the best alternative ([AI Engineer / BigGo](https://finance.biggo.com/news/0d6f6f22655ba31d)).
- **Skeptic:** OrcaRouter on Tell-1: "no model card, no API, no pricing" outside the marketing page. That's a fair warning about vendor benchmarks, though OrcaRouter is a competing router ([OrcaRouter](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/retell-tell-1-voice-model)).
