## Where the puck is going

Nothing new moves the direction set out in the last two briefs: bundled STT+TTS from a single vendor, flash/premium TTS tiers at falling prices, and turn-taking and call-state handling moving into open-source framework primitives. One framing from a recent practitioner post is worth keeping: production voice-to-voice round trips of 350–500 ms are now common, versus 700–900 ms a year ago ([slng.ai](https://slng.ai/posts/voice-ai-missing-cache-layer)). That makes raw latency table-stakes. The remaining differentiators are recovery behaviour, accuracy on hard slices (names, numbers, accents) and vertical workflow ownership.
