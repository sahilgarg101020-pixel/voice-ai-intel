## Where the puck is going

**A supervision/observability layer is starting to separate itself from the testing-tooling category this brief flagged back in June.** Coval, Cekura, Hamming and Retell's own Conductor (July 7) were framed around pre-deployment simulation and post-failure test generation. Modulate's pitch at Ai4 is adjacent but distinct: live, in-conversation monitoring for the emotional and behavioral signals — frustration, escalation risk, compliance exposure — that a transcript alone doesn't carry. It's the same underlying insight as the June 12 clinic-builder story ("turn-detection latency is the hidden variable nobody talks about, CSAT jumped 22 points") pushed one layer up the stack: once turn-taking and WER stop being the bottleneck, the next thing worth measuring is what's actually happening emotionally inside the call, in real time, not just whether the call technically completed.

**The Miravoice/Rime study is a useful data point for the "aggregate benchmarks are losing procurement relevance" thread this brief has tracked since late June.** It's not a vendor-published Elo score or WER number — it's an independent research firm running real calls and measuring the metric that actually matters commercially (did the person stay on the line, did they finish the survey). That's a meaningfully different kind of evidence than the TTS Arena leaderboard churn this brief has covered all summer (Alibaba, Grok, MiniMax, Speechify all taking turns at #1), and it reinforces a pattern: the credibility bar for any voice-AI performance claim keeps rising toward third-party, outcome-based validation rather than self-reported benchmark wins.

**Nineteen weeks of infra-layer quiet is now itself the story.** Every major independent STT/TTS vendor this brief tracks shipped something significant in the June–early July window (Cartesia's Ink-2/SIP, Deepgram's diarization and confidential-compute work, AssemblyAI's context-carryover STT, Soniox v5, Speechmatics Melia) and then went dark. Meanwhile the model labs (Microsoft's still-unannounced MAI-Realtime, xAI's Grok Voice Think Fast 2.0 default rollout) and the platform layer (Yellow.ai's SPAC, LivePerson/SoundHound) have been where the actual movement is. Worth asking whether the independent infra vendors are heads-down on something bigger, or whether the platform/model-lab consolidation this brief has tracked since late June is starting to squeeze their reason to ship standalone.
