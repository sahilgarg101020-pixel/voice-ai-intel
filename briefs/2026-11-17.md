Kotoba would become a wholly owned subsidiary, and the team reportedly stays in Japan. Nikkei frames the deal around taking Japanese content such as anime overseas. Kotoba raised a $10M seed in June, with Salesforce Ventures and Sony Innovation Fund participating ([Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/business-deals/us-ai-voice-startup-elevenlabs-to-buy-japanese-focused-peer-kotoba), [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/173172-elevenlabs-buys-japan-s-kotoba-in-100m-voice-ai-deal/)).

The TTS is a few-step diffusion model running on the NPU: speech starts in about 100ms, with 8 voices across 21 locales ([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/on-device-speech-recognition-and-text-to-speech-in-google-ai-edge-gallery/)).

## Where the puck is going

**ElevenLabs is buying non-English language depth rather than building it.** Kotoba follows the Korea launch, the Karnataka MOU, the UK GOV Voice award and the NYC Summit platform push. Each one adds a region or language to the platform. Japanese, Korean and Chinese real-time speech-to-speech translation is hard to bolt on, and it is where Chinese labs (Qwen LiveTranslate, StepFun) have been strong. A Western leader paying over $100M for a 3-year-old CJK team tells you two things. Low-latency, culturally fluent CJK voice is scarce. And the exit path for regional voice specialists is to be acquired by a global platform, not to sell API access against it. If you're a founder building a language-specific stack, that is your realistic exit. Make your data and evals in that language something a buyer can't recreate in a quarter.

**On-device speech is becoming free on mobile.** Google's ML Kit gives every Android developer free offline STT and TTS that start in about 100ms, served from the OS through AICore. It follows Apple's SpeechAnalyzer, Amazon's AZ3 wake-word chip and Underdog's CPU-only TTS. For consumer apps, the cloud STT/TTS bill on phones may go to zero for common languages. The cloud stays the better choice for agent reasoning, cloned or branded voices and telephony. Expect a split: on-device for dictation, captions and private first-pass listening, cloud for anything with a phone number or a brand voice. Vendors selling commodity mobile TTS/STT are now competing with a free OS feature.

**Legal risk is moving to the stock voice itself.** Greene getting past the demurrer means a "sounds like" claim can reach discovery even when the vendor says it hired a different actor. That's a step beyond the Netflix and Gene Wilder licensing story. Taken with the SAG-AFTRA AI terms and EU Article 50 disclosure, any stock or default voice in your product needs a documented provenance chain. Platforms with clean, provenance-tracked voice libraries now have a legal advantage as well as a quality one.
