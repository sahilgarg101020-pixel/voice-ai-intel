# Voice AI Brief — 2026-07-27

*A quiet cycle for new launches, but two threads worth a founder's attention: voice is starting to double as a control surface for other agents (Anthropic and OpenAI both shipped variants of this within 24 hours of each other), and the EU's deepfake-disclosure rules go live in six days with real fines attached.*

## TLDR

- **Anthropic and OpenAI both shipped "voice controls your other agents" this week.** Claude Voice Mode now supports Opus and Sonnet (not just Haiku) plus voice-triggered connectors into Gmail, Calendar, Slack, Canva, and Notion — but Claude Fable, Anthropic's most capable model, stays text-only; the release is tuned for daily-driver models, not frontier reasoning. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/anthropic-updates-claude-voice-mode-with-more-capable-models/)) A day later, GPT-Live landed on the ChatGPT desktop app for macOS/Windows, where it can steer Codex and ChatGPT Work agents by voice while you talk. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/openais-new-voice-mode-makes-it-to-the-chatgpt-desktop-app/))
- **SoundHound partners with Deliverect** to fully automate restaurant voice ordering — drive-through, phone, and kiosk orders route straight into kitchen POS systems across 100+ languages, no re-keying by staff. Deliverect's Don MacMillan: "Voice is the oldest ordering channel in the restaurant business, and until now it's been the least automated." ([PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/deliverect-and-soundhound-ai-partner-to-turn-voice-into-a-fully-automated-ordering-channel-for-restaurants-302833315.html))
- **EU AI Act Article 50 deepfake-disclosure rules become enforceable August 2** — six days out. Any AI-generated audio that resembles a real person now needs a disclosure label, fines run up to €15M or 3% of global turnover, and the definition is broad enough to catch voice-cloning use cases well outside the obvious deepfake cases. ([Sidley](https://datamatters.sidley.com/2026/06/24/eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-preparing-for-compliance-by-2-august-2026/))
- **Update — SoundHound/LivePerson merger**: stockholder vote now formally set for August 20; board has unanimously recommended approval. ([TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/company-announcements/liveperson-sets-august-vote-on-premium-all-stock-and-cash-merger-with-soundhound-ai))
- **Infra-layer cooldown extends past ten weeks**: still nothing from Cartesia, Deepgram, AssemblyAI, Speechmatics, or Soniox this cycle — all the motion continues to come from application/platform layers, not the core STT/TTS vendors.
- **Sourcing note**: the X/Twitter API is still returning "credits depleted" (402); this brief is sourced entirely from web search this cycle.

## Where the puck is going

**Voice is quietly becoming an input method for controlling other agents, not just a way to talk to one.** This brief has tracked "voice-as-default-assistant-furniture" since early July, but this week sharpens that into something more specific: Anthropic's Claude Voice Mode now fires Gmail/Calendar/Slack/Canva/Notion actions from a spoken command, and OpenAI's GPT-Live desktop release lets you steer Codex and ChatGPT's Work agents mid-conversation. Both vendors made the same product bet in the same 24 hours — voice as the front end for multi-step agentic work, not a standalone conversation product — while both also drew a line under it: Anthropic kept its smartest model (Fable) text-only, and OpenAI is still tuning GPT-Live's backchanneling behavior post-launch. The frontier labs seem to agree voice-driven orchestration is worth shipping now, but not yet worth routing their hardest reasoning through.

**SoundHound's Deliverect deal is a reminder that the "boring" vertical integrations are where a lot of real production volume actually lives.** It's the same playbook as Deepgram's QSR deployments in June — narrow vocabulary, high call volume, existing POS rails — arriving from the opposite direction (SoundHound is simultaneously trying to fuse voice with LivePerson's messaging platform at the enterprise-CX level while also going deep on a single, high-frequency vertical). Two different bets from one company in the same month is itself a signal that nobody's sure yet which integration depth wins.
