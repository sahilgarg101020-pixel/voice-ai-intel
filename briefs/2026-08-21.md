- **An independent benchmark (Openbenchmarks) is now publishing real-phone-call latency data for production voice agent platforms** — Telnyx fastest at 1,296ms median TTFAB (p95 1,856ms), then Bland 1,520ms, Vapi 1,558ms, Retell 1,740ms. None clear the sub-1-second bar generally cited as the threshold for feeling natural. The sharper finding: platform self-reported latency runs ~490ms below what's actually measured from the call audio, because vendor TTFB numbers typically exclude endpointing, telephony, and network delay that a real caller experiences. ([Openbenchmarks](https://openbenchmarks.com/voice-agent-latency))

## Where the puck is going

**Wispr's raise is a data point about voice-as-input, not just voice-as-output.** This brief has spent the summer on the STT/TTS leaderboard churn and session-stack bundling race (Cartesia, ElevenLabs, Deepgram) — voice-out and full-duplex infrastructure. Wispr sits on the other side: dictation as a keyboard replacement, now with its own proprietary ASR (Canto) built for the failure mode that matters for that product — noisy-environment accuracy, not raw benchmark WER. A 30%→5-10% WER cut in noise is the same "production-condition gap hidden by aggregate WER" story this brief flagged with Smallest AI in June, now funding a $2B round instead of a benchmark post. The wearable partnership (silent dictation via a ring) also belongs on the on-device/edge thread — voice input heading toward contexts where speaking aloud isn't an option, a different engineering problem than agent turn-taking.

**The Openbenchmarks numbers matter less for the rankings than for existing at all.** This brief's "benchmark claims need receipts" thread (Miravoice's caller-retention study, Google's unfalsifiable 63%-voice-usage stat two weeks ago) has been about the absence of independently measured, real-call data in a market that's otherwise all vendor self-reporting. A shop measuring TTFAB from saved call audio — finding a consistent ~490ms gap versus vendor-reported numbers — is a concrete third-party instrument for closing that gap, not another complaint about it. For a founder evaluating a platform, "vendor's number minus ~500ms" is now a usable heuristic rather than a guess.

**The SoundHound-LivePerson stumble is a small data point for the roll-up thesis tracked since OVH-Gladia and Poland/ElevenLabs.** Overwhelming *support* (97%+ of votes cast) failing to clear an *outstanding-shares* quorum threshold isn't a deal in trouble — it's a reminder that public-company merger mechanics (retail turnout, proxy logistics) are real friction even with full institutional backing. Worth watching whether the extra two weeks closes the gap.
