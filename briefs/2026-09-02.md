## Where the puck is going

**The Speech Agent Arena numbers are the first hard, outcome-based ceiling this brief has seen on agentic voice performance — and they land well under the demo-day story.** This brief has tracked the "benchmark claims need receipts" thread since August (Miravoice's hang-up-rate study, the Openbenchmarks ~490ms real-vs-claimed latency gap) and the "demo-to-production gap" thread since June (60% failure rate, 74% enterprise rollback). Task Success Rate is the first number that connects the two directly: even the model xAI itself has marketed hardest on agentic capability completes barely half its scenarios end-to-end, and the field drops into the high-30s from there. For a founder benchmarking a model choice, Arena Preference (voice quality) and Task Success Rate (did it actually do the thing) are now visibly different rankings — cite both, or the claim is incomplete.

**Blue Voice is a clean instance of the "context is the moat, not the model" thesis this brief has tracked since Gradium's Zeghidour named it in early August.** A police-policy assistant isn't a better STT/TTS stack — it's a narrow, deeply-integrated retrieval layer (municipal and state legal databases) wrapped in a conversational interface, in a vertical no existing voice-AI vendor has touched. That's the same shape as HappyRobot's six-model coordination thesis and Prosper AI's healthcare play: the defensible ground in voice AI keeps turning out to be domain-specific context and workflow integration, not synthesis quality, which is exactly what the Speech Agent Arena numbers above are also implying from the benchmark side.

**Everything else is where it was yesterday.** Murf's $0.01/min Falcon 2 is now 13 days old with zero incumbent price response. The bundle-into-the-platform thread (Mavenir, Krisp, Speechmatics/LiveKit) hasn't added a fourth node this cycle.
