## Where the puck is going

**The SoundHound–LivePerson vote is worth treating as more than a calendar item — it's the first up-or-down market test of a consolidation thesis this brief has been assembling in pieces since June.** OVH's acquisition of Gladia closed August 3. Poland took an equity stake in ElevenLabs in June. Yellow.ai's SPAC proceeds are earmarked to buy BPO operators outright. Now SoundHound and LivePerson — voice-agentic AI merging with enterprise digital messaging — have cleared every regulatory hurdle and go to a vote with unanimous recommendations behind them. The pattern across all of these: as model-layer quality keeps churning on a weekly cycle (three different #1 TTS models from three countries in one month back in June, still no durable leader since), capital is betting the defensible position is owned distribution and channel depth, not any particular model. A "for" vote Wednesday would be the clearest signal yet that public-market capital agrees with what private rounds have been pricing in since ElevenLabs' valuation doubled in five months.

**The absence of anything else this cycle is itself a data point worth naming plainly rather than papering over.** Three days of searching vendor blogs, changelogs, HN, and general coverage turned up nothing that wasn't already in this brief under a different headline, or wasn't actually a year-old story getting re-surfaced by search indexing (the Google agentic-calling feature is the clearest example — it's a real and interesting product, just not new). That's a useful reminder for the vendor-changelog habit this brief has recommended before: infra teams are very likely still shipping quietly even when there's no press-worthy launch to point to, which is exactly the kind of week where checking a changelog directly beats waiting for it to surface in coverage.
