## Where the puck is going

**SaaS incumbents are buying their way into voice.** Monday.com acquired a small voice team in May and shipped a product five months later. This is the same distribution-incumbent pattern the tracker has followed (Fireflies, Gupshup, Intermedia): the buyer already owns the CRM and workflow data, so a standalone sales-call voice vendor competes against a bundled default. Differentiation has to come from depth the incumbent won't build.

**Domain-specific STT is the other way to compete.** Speechmatics is marketing recall on medical terms rather than aggregate WER, which continues its error-class framing from September and sits next to Deepgram's air-gapped push as vendors move to narrow, high-stakes slices. The number needs independent replication, since the comparison set and test data are the vendor's own.

**Carrier-grade summarization is a quiet channel.** Deutsche Telekom shipping call notes through AudioCodes suggests telcos will resell voice AI as a feature of the line, another distribution route that bypasses API-first vendors.
