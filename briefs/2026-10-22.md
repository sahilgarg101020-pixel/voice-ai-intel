## TLDR

- **Sierra launched fleming-1 on Oct 7, a model that scores a caller's audio in real time for whether the voice is synthetic.** It is tuned conservatively so real people aren't flagged, only returns a score (the business decides the response), and works on phone calls only. It pairs with Sierra's Personal Agent Protocol, which lets agents declare themselves. No accuracy figures published ([Sierra](https://sierra.ai/blog/caller-id-in-the-age-of-agents), [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/sierra-fleming-1)).
- **Decagon introduced Voice 3 and Chord at Dialogues on Oct 1.** Voice 3 is a duplex-architecture agent in 70+ languages. Chord is Decagon's first in-house speech model, post-trained on CX conversations, adjusting pace mid-sentence (slower for codes and phone numbers). Decagon says it is trained on licensed data and consented talent, not customer data. Its own 185-listener test puts Chord first at 36.2% ([Decagon](https://decagon.ai/blog/decagon-chord-voice-model), [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/decagon-voice-3)).
- **Update (no new bullet):** Mistral Voxtral 3 and Cartesia Sonic-3.6 coverage repeats facts already reported.

## Where the puck is going

**Application-layer vendors are training their own voice models.** Decagon (Chord) follows the pattern of agent platforms moving down the stack, as Sierra did with AIUC-1 and its own voice work. Owning the TTS lets them tune for CX specifics (digit pacing, consented voices) and for provenance claims. For a founder building on third-party TTS, "licensed and consented data" is becoming a procurement talking point you may need an answer for.

**Synthetic-caller detection is now a product category.** Fleming-1 treats inbound AI callers as a threat class, expecting personal agents to be a large share of calls. That connects to the voice-cloning attack thread (Teams enterprise attack) and to the call-for-me products already in market. The open design question is who declares what: a self-identification protocol only covers cooperative agents, so detection fills the rest.

**Pace and prosody are shifting from global settings to context-aware control.** Chord slowing for codes and phone numbers is the TTS-side match to STT vendors marketing against digit and negation errors. The error classes that cost money in production are getting named on both sides of the pipeline.
