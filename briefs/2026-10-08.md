## Where the puck is going

No change to the direction set over the past two weeks. Bundled single-vendor stacks (STT + LLM + TTS under one key, e.g. [Deepgram's Voice Agent API](https://deepgram.com) and Cartesia Line) keep absorbing the pipeline, and hyperscalers are pricing transcription and TTS aggressively. Per-minute headline prices are also getting less informative: one analysis argues a "$0.05/min" Vapi call bills at [$0.09–$0.14](https://beri.net/article/voice-ai-per-minute-pricing-vapi-retell-elevenlabs-bland-cost-per-resolved-call) once providers are stacked, which supports the outcome-based pricing thread (SquadStack, Sept 15).

For a builder: raw latency is table-stakes. The open ground is recovery behaviour, accuracy on names/numbers/accents, transparent all-in pricing and owning a vertical workflow end to end.
