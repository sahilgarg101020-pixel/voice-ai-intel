## Where the puck is going

**Speech-to-speech is splitting by how much control the buyer gets.** Opaque end-to-end S2S models (GPT-Live, Eleven Realtime) won on latency. The new entrants are adding back the control points regulated buyers asked for:
- Rime adds a text checkpoint before audio.
- PolyAI's Dialog-RSN-1 takes audio in but hands speech generation to a separate TTS.
- Phonic sells a full S2S model as an API.

This continues the Oct 23 theme that S2S opacity drives demand for transcripts and logging. Vendors are now building that inspection layer into the model, not leaving it to logging tools. If you sell into finance or healthcare, plan on this mix: S2S for timing, plus a text layer you can audit.

**STT vendors are becoming full-stack.** AssemblyAI adding TTS follows Deepgram's Flux TTS. Pricing per character with no contract puts pressure on ElevenLabs' Turbo tier at the low end. Guava's pitch is the same at the startup level: one in-house stack instead of stitched vendors. The middle of the pipeline is consolidating from both ends.

**Benchmarks and preference are coming apart.** Artificial Analysis reportedly ranked Phonic-1 first on its Speech-to-Speech Index but eighth in blind live-conversation preference ([Digg](https://digg.com/ai/g3sw5lrd)). Benchmark wins are not the same as callers preferring the voice. Test with real callers, not leaderboards.

**Deadlines are piling up.** Hume's API ends Nov 13, Gemini 3.1 Flash Live Nov 17, Cartesia's hosted Line migration Dec 1 and Vertex native audio Dec 13. The pattern from the last three weeks is unchanged: abstract your model IDs, or you will spend sprints chasing deprecation dates.

## Signals from the field

- **Benchmark vs preference:** Phonic-1 is reportedly "narrowly first" on the Artificial Analysis S2S Index but eighth in blind preference ([Digg](https://digg.com/ai/g3sw5lrd)).
- **Commentator on AssemblyAI-TTS-1:** it is "competitive on accuracy and probably cheap," but not clearly top on listener preference ([AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/assemblyai-s-tts-1-beats-elevenlabs-on-speed-with-47ms-first-audio)). One third-party test (Cekura) puts it first on reading phone numbers and codes, at 91.7%.
