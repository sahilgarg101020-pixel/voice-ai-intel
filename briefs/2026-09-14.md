## Where the puck is going

**Apple's Audio Intelligence is a hardware answer to a question this brief's voice-as-default-interface thread hasn't directly asked yet: what does "always listening" cost in silicon, not just in model quality?** Live Rewind and Siri Recap require new secure hardware specifically to process ambient audio continuously without the transcript leaving the device or getting stored wholesale — a different engineering bar than the latency/turn-taking work that's dominated this brief since June. For founders building anything closer to "ambient assistant" than "call-in agent" (meeting note-takers, wearables, always-on companions), the constraint isn't model latency, it's proving to a privacy-cautious user (and increasingly a privacy-cautious platform) that raw audio never has to leave a trust boundary. Plaud and other wearable note-takers now have a much bigger, better-funded competitor defining what "responsible ambient audio" looks like at consumer scale.

**Gupshup entering voice is another instance of the distribution-incumbent-undercuts-the-specialists pattern** this brief flagged with Fireflies (Sept 3) — a company with existing enterprise relationships and infrastructure (there, meeting notes; here, WhatsApp/RCS/SMS at CPaaS scale) bolts on voice-agent capability at a price the dedicated platforms can't easily match, because the calling infrastructure and customer relationship are already sunk costs. $0.035/min is aggressive enough that it's worth watching whether Vapi, Retell, or Bland respond on price rather than just on feature depth — the TTS price war this brief has tracked since Murf's Falcon 2 (Aug 28) may be about to show up one layer up, at the agent-platform level.
